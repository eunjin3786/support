Oshito

Make a cutout of your fave from a photo, place it on the live camera, and take pictures together.

Features

✂️

Auto Cutout

Pick a photo and the background is removed automatically, right on your device.

📸

Snap Together

Place your fave on the live camera — drag to move, pinch and twist to resize and rotate.

🪞

Mirror

Flip your fave so they face the right way.

💬

Speech Bubbles

Add a little message from your fave.

🖼️

Library Photos

Put your fave on a photo from your library instead of the camera.

⏱️

Self-timer

Set a 3 or 10 second timer to get into the shot.

Tips

🧍

Clear subjects work best

Background removal works best when the person or character is clearly visible and not too small.

🗑️

Up to 15 faves

You can save up to 15 cutouts. Long-press a cutout in the list to delete it.

🔒

Camera access

If the camera shows nothing, allow camera access in Settings > Oshito.
